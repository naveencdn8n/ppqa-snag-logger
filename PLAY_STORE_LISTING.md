## Full Description (4000 chars max)

```
PPQA Snag Logger is a professional construction quality assurance app built for site inspection teams. Designed for Pre-Possession Quality Audits (PPQA), it gives inspectors, supervisors, and project managers a fast, structured way to log defects and track their resolution — all in real time.

━━ WHO IS IT FOR? ━━
• Site Inspectors – Log snags on the go with photos and videos
• Supervisors – Monitor team progress and assign tasks
• QA Managers – Generate reports and export data for management review
• Developers & PMCs – Get a real-time view of open defects by tower, floor, and unit

━━ KEY FEATURES ━━

📋 Fast Snag Logging
Log defects in seconds. Select tower, floor, unit, room, element, and trade. Attach photos or videos as evidence. Classify by severity: Red Line, Major, or Minor.

📍 Structured Location Hierarchy
Organise every snag by Tower → Floor → Unit → Room so nothing gets missed and every defect is traceable to its exact location.

🎯 Severity & Status Tracking
Three severity levels (Red Line, Major, Minor) and four statuses (Open, In Progress, Closed, Disputed) keep your team aligned on priorities.

📊 Tower Reports
See open vs. closed snag counts per tower, drill down to floor and unit level. Know exactly which units are ready for handover at a glance.

✅ My Tasks
Inspectors see their assigned snags and can update status directly from their task list — no need to search through the full snag register.

🔢 Stable Serial Numbers
Every snag gets a permanent #N reference number (oldest = #1) so teams can reference snags in meetings, emails, and reports without confusion.

📷 Photo & Video Evidence
Attach multiple photos or short videos to each snag. Evidence is stored securely in the cloud and visible to all authorised team members.

🌐 Web Admin Portal
Project managers get a full-featured web portal to manage projects, team members, defect libraries, locations, and detailed reports — all at ppqa-snag-logger.web.app.

📤 Google Sheets Export
Export the full snag register to Google Sheets in one tap for management reporting, distribution, and offline analysis.

🔒 Role-Based Access
Inspectors, Supervisors, Admins, and View-Only roles — each person sees only what they need. Admins manage the team via the web portal.

📶 Works Offline
Log snags without a network connection. All data syncs automatically when connectivity is restored.

━━ BUILT FOR THE INDUSTRY ━━
PPQA Snag Logger replaces paper checklists, WhatsApp groups, and sprawling Excel sheets with a structured, accountable digital workflow. Every defect is logged with inspector name, timestamp, location, and evidence — creating an auditable trail from first inspection to final handover.

━━ GETTING STARTED ━━
Your organisation administrator will send you an invite. Once you sign in, you are automatically assigned to your project and can start logging immediately.

For organisations interested in onboarding their team, visit ppqa-snag-logger.web.app or contact us at privacy@ppqa.in.

━━ DATA & PRIVACY ━━
All data is stored securely on Google Firebase. Access is restricted to authorised team members only. No data is sold to third parties. Full privacy policy: ppqa-snag-logger.web.app/privacy-policy.html
```
